# Similar results: on-treatment blood-test changes and survival

Written 2026-10-09 from a full reading of eight studies. Each study was opened at its source; access is stated per study. This file holds published group-level findings only. It contains no index-patient value and must never be used as an individual prognosis.

Labels: **verified** means seen on a page or file that was opened; **not reported** means the study does not state it; **inferred** means our own reasoning or arithmetic.

## Terms used below

- **PSA**: prostate-specific antigen, a blood protein made by prostate tissue and prostate cancer. It tracks tumour activity.
- **ALP**: alkaline phosphatase, a blood enzyme that rises when bone is being rebuilt, for example around bone metastases.
- **ADT**: androgen deprivation therapy, hormone therapy that removes testosterone.
- **Castration-resistant**: the cancer grows despite testosterone being suppressed. **Hormone-sensitive**: it still shrinks when testosterone is suppressed.
- **Hazard ratio (HR)**: the ratio of death rates between two groups at any moment. 0.43 means 57 per cent lower; 1.69 means 69 per cent higher.
- **Median overall survival**: the time by which half of a group has died.
- **Landmark analysis**: survival is counted from a fixed checkpoint, among only the patients alive then, after each patient's group has been fixed. It prevents guarantee-time bias, the artificial advantage a group gets when its members had to survive long enough to qualify for it.
- **Surrogate**: an early marker that could replace survival as a trial's endpoint. Being prognostic, linked to survival, is a weaker claim than being a surrogate.

## 1. Petrylak et al. 2006, SWOG 99-16

Citation: Petrylak DP, Ankerst DP, Jiang CS, et al. J Natl Cancer Inst 2006;98(8):516-521. DOI 10.1093/jnci/djj129. PMID 16622120. Full text read from an Internet Archive copy of the publisher page; tables 1 to 4 and the figure were not archived.

- **Who (verified):** 674 eligible men with castration-resistant disease, docetaxel plus estramustine against mitoxantrone plus prednisone. 551 analysed at 3 months, 532 at 2 months, 456 at 1 month. Men without a baseline PSA or a later PSA in the window were excluded.
- **What was measured (verified):** PSA only, requested every 3 weeks. Baseline was the last PSA before treatment, required within 28 days of entry. A decline counted if the **lowest** PSA anywhere in the window fell by the threshold; one unconfirmed value was enough. PSA velocity, the slope of log PSA over time, was also tested.
- **How they found it (verified):** three Cox regressions: treatment only, the PSA marker only, and both. A marker passed the Prentice criteria for a surrogate when treatment mattered alone, the marker mattered alone, and with both in the model the marker still mattered while treatment no longer did. They also computed the proportion of treatment effect explained (PTE) and required its lower 95 per cent bound above 0.5. Models were adjusted for baseline PSA and prior prostatectomy. The chosen threshold had the highest lower PTE bound.
- **What they found (verified):** a fall of 30 per cent or more within 3 months gave HR 0.43 (0.34 to 0.55), P < .001, and 0.40 after adjusting for treatment. It was reached by 76 per cent on docetaxel against 40 per cent on mitoxantrone. Falls of 20 to 40 per cent met all criteria at 3 months; 50 per cent narrowly failed. At **2 months** the 30 per cent fall met all criteria (PTE 1.0, 0.62 to 1.0); at 1 month it failed. PSA velocity met the criteria at 2 and 3 months.
- **Limits (verified):** retrospective; the threshold came from the same data and needs prospective validation; "a statistical surrogate does not constitute a clinical surrogate"; specific to cell-killing chemotherapy. **Inferred:** survival was not counted from a landmark, so responders may carry guarantee-time advantage.
- **To compare one patient:** the last PSA before the first chemotherapy dose, that date, and every dated PSA in the first 2 to 3 months, because the lowest value is used.

## 2. Armstrong et al. 2007, TAX327

Citation: Armstrong AJ, Garrett-Mayer E, Ou Yang YC, et al. J Clin Oncol 2007;25(25):3965-3970. DOI 10.1200/JCO.2007.11.4769. PMID 17761981. Erratum J Clin Oncol 2008;26(5):820. Full text read from an Internet Archive copy, with tables.

- **Who (verified):** 989 treated men with castration-resistant disease: docetaxel every 3 weeks (329), weekly docetaxel (327) or mitoxantrone (333), all with prednisone.
- **What was measured (verified):** PSA and pain every 3 weeks. Decline was baseline against the lowest PSA within 3 months, one unconfirmed value enough. The baseline value used is not reported.
- **How they found it (verified):** the same three-model Prentice approach as Petrylak; thresholds tested from 0 to 90 per cent in 10 per cent steps; the highest PTE chosen.
- **What they found (verified):** a fall of 30 per cent or more: HR 0.50 (0.43 to 0.57), median 21.6 against 13.0 months, PTE 0.66 (0.23 to 1.0). Every threshold's lower PTE bound was below 0.5, "modest surrogacy". Medians by threshold, all three arms pooled: 0 per cent 20.7 against 11.7 months; 50 per cent 22.4 against 14.6; 90 per cent 26.6 against 17.4.
- **Limits (verified):** only two thirds of the survival difference explained; weekly docetaxel lowered PSA more without longer survival; "not generalizable to different stages of prostate cancer"; a 30 per cent rule "may be incorrect in nearly one third of cases".
- **To compare one patient:** pre-treatment PSA, docetaxel start date and schedule, and all PSA values over the first 3 months.

## 3. Sonpavde et al. 2012, TAX327 alkaline phosphatase

Citation: Sonpavde G, et al. Urol Oncol 2012;30(5):607-613. DOI 10.1016/j.urolonc.2010.07.002. PMID 20888271. **Abstract only**: the publisher returned HTTP 403 and no open copy exists.

- **Who (verified):** 601 TAX327 men with castration-resistant disease, bone metastases, baseline ALP of **120 U/L or more** and at least two later ALP values.
- **What was measured (verified):** ALP normalization, below 120 U/L by day 90, and an ALP increase by day 90. The increase threshold and day 0 are not reported in the abstract.
- **How they found it (verified):** Kaplan-Meier and Cox regression; each ALP change adjusted for one PSA change; surrogacy tested with the likelihood reduction factor.
- **What they found (verified):** 159 normalized and 442 did not. Normalization HR 0.79 (0.65 to 0.97) after adjusting for a PSA fall of 30 per cent; increase HR 1.69 (1.33 to 2.14) after adjusting for a PSA rise of 50 per cent. ALP change was not a valid surrogate.
- **Limits:** the requirement of two post-treatment values drops early deaths (inferred). The authors call for validation.
- **To compare one patient:** castration-resistant disease, bone metastases and a **raised** baseline ALP. A man whose ALP was normal at the start is outside this population.

## 4. Hussain et al. 2006, SWOG 9346

Citation: Hussain M, et al. J Clin Oncol 2006;24(24):3984-3990. DOI 10.1200/JCO.2006.06.4246. PMID 16921051. Full text read from an Internet Archive copy; its tables and figures were not archived.

- **Who (verified):** newly metastatic men with PSA of 5 ng/mL or more, treated with 7 months of goserelin plus bicalutamide, no chemotherapy. 1,345 entered the survival analysis.
- **What was measured (verified):** PSA at months 6 and 7. Groups were 0.2 or below ("undetectable"), above 0.2 to 4, and above 4 ng/mL. A missing value in the window counted as not reaching 4 or below.
- **How they found it (verified):** survival counted from month 7, excluding men last seen before then, which is a landmark design; Kaplan-Meier and stepwise Cox regression adjusted for performance status, Gleason sum, bone pain and entry PSA.
- **What they found (verified):** median survival 75 months (602 men) at 0.2 or below, 44 months (360) above 0.2 to 4, 13 months (383) above 4. Adjusted HR against the above-4 group: 0.17 (0.13 to 0.21) and 0.30 (0.24 to 0.38). 81 per cent reached 4 or below at some point.
- **Limits:** treatment era of the 1990s with hormone therapy only; the above-4 group mixes missing values with true failure (inferred).
- **To compare one patient:** hormone therapy start date and PSA at months 6 and 7. A man who also received docetaxel is only loosely comparable.

## 5. Harshman et al. 2018, CHAARTED

Citation: Harshman LC, et al. J Clin Oncol 2018;36(4):376-382. DOI 10.1200/JCO.2017.75.3921. PMID 29261442. PMC5805480. Full text and tables read.

- **Who (verified):** 790 CHAARTED men enrolled 2006 to 2012, hormone therapy plus six docetaxel cycles against hormone therapy alone; hormone therapy could start up to 120 days before randomization. 719 analysed (358 docetaxel, 361 hormone therapy alone). Excluded: 14 deaths and 14 withdrawals before month 7, 8 with under 7 months of follow-up, 35 with no PSA in months 4 to 7. High volume meant four or more bone lesions with at least one outside the spine and pelvis, or any organ metastasis.
- **What was measured (verified):** PSA from **months 4 to 7 after hormone therapy started**, in the SWOG 9346 groups. The rule for choosing among several values in the window is not reported. Men who progressed or started non-protocol therapy before month 7 were placed in the above-4 group.
- **How they found it (verified):** landmark at month 7; Kaplan-Meier with stratified log-rank tests for everyone and for each arm; Cox regression stratified by randomization factors, built stepwise; logistic regression for what predicted reaching 0.2 or below.
- **What they found (verified):**

| Arm | PSA group | Men (deaths) | Median survival, months | 3-year survival |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All | 0.2 or below | 266 (38) | 60.4 | 77.7% |
| All | above 0.2 to 4 | 214 (53) | 51.9 | 61.4% |
| All | above 4 or failing | 239 (123) | 22.2 | 31.5% |
| Hormone therapy plus docetaxel | 0.2 or below | 162 (25) | 60.4 | 74.2% |
| Hormone therapy plus docetaxel | above 0.2 to 4 | 109 (29) | 45.5 | 62.2% |
| Hormone therapy plus docetaxel | above 4 or failing | 87 (39) | 25.2 | 36.8% |

  In the docetaxel arm 45.3 per cent reached 0.2 or below, 30.4 per cent were above 0.2 to 4 and 24.3 per cent above 4 or failing. Adjusted HR against the above-4 group: 0.18 (0.12 to 0.28) for 0.2 or below and 0.33 (0.23 to 0.47) for above 0.2 to 4.
- **Limits (verified):** retrospective; younger, fitter trial men (median age 63); requiring survival to month 7 favours better-risk men; the groups already differed before treatment, for example low volume in 50.4 against 16.3 per cent and median baseline PSA 16.9 against 191.0 ng/mL. **Inferred:** the above-4 group mixes high PSA with early treatment failure, so a man with a slowly falling PSA just above 4 is not typical of it.
- **To compare one patient:** hormone therapy start date; every dated PSA in months 4 to 7; alive and not progressing at month 7; docetaxel start and number of cycles; disease volume; baseline PSA. Before month 7 no group applies.

## 6. Saad et al. 2024, ARASENS

Citation: Saad F, et al. Eur Urol 2024;86(4):329-339. DOI 10.1016/j.eururo.2024.03.036. PMID 38644146. Full text and supplement read.

- **Who (verified):** 1,305 men, hormone therapy plus six docetaxel cycles, with darolutamide (651) or placebo (654). Hormone therapy could start up to 12 weeks before randomization. Performance status 0 to 1.
- **What was measured (verified):** PSA every 12 weeks. Undetectable meant below 0.2 ng/mL on two measurements at least 3 weeks apart. Time points 12, 24, 36 and 52 weeks, implicitly from randomization.
- **How they found it (verified):** post hoc landmark Cox regression at 24 and 36 weeks, stratified only by randomization factors, each arm separately.
- **What they found (verified):** below 0.2 at 24 weeks in 49 per cent with darolutamide against **24 per cent with hormone therapy plus docetaxel alone**. In the hormone therapy plus docetaxel arm, below 0.2 against not: HR 0.36 (0.25 to 0.51) at 24 weeks and 0.34 (0.24 to 0.49) at 36 weeks; median survival of the not-below group 36.9 months, clock start not stated. Darolutamide arm at 24 weeks: HR 0.49 (0.37 to 0.65).
- **Limits (verified):** post hoc, no adjustment beyond randomization factors; men reaching undetectable PSA started with lower PSA and ALP and less high-volume disease.
- **To compare one patient:** treatment start dates, PSA near 12, 24 and 36 weeks with a confirming value, the laboratory's reporting limit.

## 7. Wallis et al. 2026, ARASENS and ARANOTE

Citation: Wallis CJD, et al. Eur Urol Oncol 2026;9(2):251-258. DOI 10.1016/j.euo.2025.03.001. PMID 40312181. **Abstract, statistical appendix and supplementary table only**; the main text was blocked.

- **Who (verified):** ARASENS (1,305) and ARANOTE (669, hormone therapy with or without darolutamide, no docetaxel); men with complete data.
- **What was measured (verified):** every PSA value from randomization, log-transformed, fitted as a smooth curve per man in a mixed model. Two features entered survival: the model's starting log-PSA and the current slope of the log-PSA curve.
- **How they found it (verified):** a joint model, fitting the PSA curves and survival together, with a Weibull baseline death rate, with and without adjustment for treatment, age, volume, visceral disease, de novo presentation and Gleason grade. No landmark; every value is used.
- **What they found (verified):** HR 0.71 (0.69 to 0.74) in ARASENS for each extra ln 2 of log-PSA decline per time unit, a 29 per cent lower death rate; 0.75 in ARANOTE. **Inferred from the supplement:** the hormone therapy plus docetaxel arm alone gives about 0.73. A curve that flattens loses this benefit, and a rising curve carries risk.
- **Limits:** the model's coefficients and time unit are not published, so no individual can be placed on it (verified absence).
- **To compare one patient:** not possible quantitatively. Qualitatively, a steeper sustained fall is better.

## 8. Salfi et al. 2025, de novo hormone-sensitive, routine blood tests

Citation: Salfi G, et al. Cancers (Basel) 2025;17(23):3806. DOI 10.3390/cancers17233806. PMID 41375007. PMC12691422. Full text and 29-page supplement read.

- **Who (verified):** 363 consecutive Swiss patients 2014 to 2023, de novo metastatic hormone-sensitive, performance status 0 to 1. Hormone therapy alone 245, plus a newer hormone tablet 82, docetaxel-based 36. Median follow-up 69.5 months.
- **What was measured (verified):** baseline closest to the first hormone dose, within 28 days, then every value up to 7 months plus 28 days for 18 routine tests. Each change graded on a side-effect scale and analysed as any grade against none. Key definitions are **fixed cut-offs**: ALP increase means reaching 130 to 325 U/L from a baseline below 130, or doubling from a higher baseline; low albumin means below 40 g/L; low sodium means 130 to 135 mmol/L; neutropenia below 2.0 x10^9/L; low lymphocytes 0.8 to 1.5 x10^9/L.
- **How they found it (verified):** Kaplan-Meier and univariable Cox for each of 26 events; those with P below 0.10 entered one multivariable Cox model with 7-month PSA and treatment. No correction for multiple testing; no external validation; the model omitted disease volume.
- **What they found (verified):** ALP increase HR 1.93 (1.18 to 3.17), low albumin 1.92 (1.18 to 3.13), low sodium 1.79 (1.05 to 3.04). Not significant: neutropenia 1.67 (0.86 to 3.24), low lymphocytes P = 0.324, and 21 other events including haemoglobin, platelets, liver enzymes, creatinine and CRP. PSA below 0.2 at 7 months: HR 0.15 (0.07 to 0.30).
- **Limits (verified):** single centre, retrospective, small, mixed treatments, up to 30 per cent missing data, uncorrected testing; several internal inconsistencies in reported P values and counts.
- **To compare one patient:** hormone therapy start date, every value of ALP, albumin, sodium, neutrophils and lymphocytes through month 7, laboratory units and ranges matching the study's cut-offs.

## What these studies teach this project

1. **Definitions differ and change the answer.** Lowest value within a window (Petrylak, Armstrong) is not the value at a checkpoint (Hussain, Harshman), and a single value is not a confirmed one (Saad). Every feature in our pipeline must state which it is.
2. **Most of them used a landmark** (Hussain, Harshman, Saad); the two surrogacy papers did not. Our D-037 and D-038 landmark design follows the stronger practice.
3. **Failing patients are often folded into the worst group** (Harshman, Hussain). Our flow diagrams must report early deaths and early failures separately.
4. **A 2-month PSA signal exists in castration-resistant docetaxel data** (Petrylak), and DREAM's laboratory record covers about 84 days, so a cycle 3 or 4 landmark can test it directly.
5. **Nothing here is causal or individual.** These are group associations from selected trial or hospital populations.
